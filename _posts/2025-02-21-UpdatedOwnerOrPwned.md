# Owner or Pwned? 
Discovering and Remediating AD Object Ownership Issues

## Abstract
The default behavior in Active Directory allows the Owner of an AD Object to fully
control that Object. Do you know who owns objects in your AD Forest? Do you know
which AD Object Owners could compromise your AD Forest? Do you know who could
own your AD Objects and who could Pwn your AD?
